Legend & Instructions

Desktop (Keyboard Shortcuts): Move the mouse and press [S] to place a rock, [F] for a leaf, [P] to slide the snake at cursor height, and [Space] to clear the pond.

Mobile / Click: Click or tap on the pond to spawn a fly, or use the buttons in the menu.

🪰 Fly: Recognized by the Net Convexity Detector (small, dark, fast). Triggers Taddeo's ballistic tongue strike.

🍃 Leaf: Triggers the Moving Edge Detector, but not the Bug Detector (too large/slow for an attack).

🪨 Rock: Once stationary on the bottom, Stationary Background Suppression erases it from retinal vision.

🐍 Snake: Triggers the Net Dimming Detector (sudden drop in light and large silhouette), causing Taddeo to dive underwater.

Taddeo (1959)

"What the Frog's Eye Tells the Frog's Brain"

In 1959 at MIT, Jerome Lettvin and his colleagues discovered that the frog's retina does not transmit a raw "photograph" to the brain, but acts as an integrated parallel computer.

The retina filters the visual world by extracting only signals crucial for survival: it erases stationary backgrounds, ignores slow leaves, and triggers an attack exclusively on small, fast-moving dark spots.
